# Outreach Email Templates

> Companion to `erie-outreach-contacts.md`. One template per audience type. **Replace every `[bracketed]` field**, keep it short, and make one **low-friction** ask — a link to try or a one-word reply, **never a scheduled call**.
>
> **Sending tips**
> - **Personalize the first line** (mention their school/course/team by name) — it's the difference between a reply and the trash.
> - **One ask per email:** a **low-friction async step** — try the free link or reply with one word. **No scheduling, no calls or video.**
> - **Prepare three reusable assets once**, then point every email at them: a **self-serve free-access link**, a short **visual walkthrough** (3–5 **annotated screenshots**), and a **one-page PDF overview**. Built once, they replace every call.
> - **Images, not video:** keep them light — **link** to the screenshots (or a small PDF), or inline **no more than 1–2**. Heavy inline images get blocked by default in some clients and can trip spam filters; always add descriptive **alt text**.
> - **Follow up once** ~5–7 days later if no reply, then stop.
> - **Cold B2B compliance:** email from a real address, use your real name + org, and add a one-line opt-out (included below). Don't blast a big BCC list — send individually or mail-merge.
> - **Signature block** (reuse on all):
>   ```
>   [Your Name] · Founder, Debate Dissector
>   [website] · [email]
>   Try it free (no login wall): [free-access link] · See it in action: [screenshots link]
>   ```
