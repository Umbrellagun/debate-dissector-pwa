# Erie Market Outreach — Contact Checklist

> **Scope:** Phase 1 launch market = **Erie City / Erie County, PA**, plus a few just-outside-county programs in PHSSL District 1 and nearby colleges. The NJ home market is listed at the bottom for Phase 2.
>
> **How to use this list:** These are the *institutions* and the *right role* to contact, each with an official directory link so you can grab the **current** email. A few professional emails are already published publicly — those are included and marked ✅. Everything else is marked 🔎 (look it up on the linked page). **Always verify the address before sending, personalize every email (see `outreach-email-templates.md`), and don't bulk-blast.**
>
> **Legend:** ✅ published email included · 🔎 find current email at the linked directory · ☎ phone
>
> **Fit note:** Debate Dissector fits **grades ~7–12 and college** best. Elementary "grade schools" aren't a target — reach all grade levels through the **district curriculum office** (Section 3).
