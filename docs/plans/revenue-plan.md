## What's Missing or Weak
1. Customer discovery before pricing — The price tiers are guesses right now. Before locking them in, you need 5–10 conversations with debate coaches to understand budget authority, purchasing processes, and willingness to pay. Add a validation step before Phase 2 pricing.

2. Grant timelines are vague — "Not applied" doesn't help you act. ECGRA Ignite Erie has specific application windows (usually spring/fall). Erie Community Foundation has quarterly deadlines. The plan should map to actual grant cycles so you don't miss them.

3. Distribution mechanics — How do students actually get the app? School IT departments are gatekeepers. You need to address: PWA install flow, Chromebook compatibility (most Erie schools are 1:1 Chromebooks), whether IT needs to whitelist the domain, COPPA/FERPA compliance documentation ready before approaching schools.

4. Erie-specific funding you're missing:

Jefferson Educational Society — actively funds civic education projects in Erie
Erie Innovation District / Flagship Opportunity Zone — tech startup incentives
Edinboro University / Penn State Behrend — academic partnerships, student interns, institutional pilot sites
United Way of Erie County — education initiative funding
Erie Regional Chamber — startup spotlight / pitch competitions
5. No competitive positioning — A grant reviewer or school admin will ask "why not just use Google Docs + a rubric?" You need a one-paragraph answer in this plan.

6. Sustainability gap — What happens when Year 1 grant money is spent? The plan jumps from "free pilots" to "$800/school" without explaining how you convert free users to paying customers when the grant expires.

7. Who does the work? — Is this just you? Approaching 6+ schools, writing 4+ grant applications, and building features simultaneously is a lot for one person. Consider noting whether you need a co-founder, part-time biz dev help, or a Gannon SBDC advisor.

8. Impact measurement for grants — Grant applications need measurable outcomes. Add specific KPIs: "Students using Debate Dissector will identify 30% more logical fallacies in practice debates" or similar.
